Source: Corporate Finance Institute (CFI)
URL: https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/accounting/ebitda-margin/
Metric: EBITDA Margin

PRIMARY FORMULA:
EBITDA Margin = EBITDA / Net Sales

ALTERNATIVE EXPRESSION:
EBITDA Margin = EBITDA / Revenue

COMPONENT FORMULA:
EBITDA = Operating Income (EBIT) + Depreciation + Amortization
EBIT = Revenue - COGS - Operating Expenses

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- EBITDA: Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization
- Net Sales / Revenue: Total sales revenue from the income statement
- EBIT: Operating income (earnings before interest and tax)
- Depreciation: Non-cash expense reducing asset value over time
- Amortization: Non-cash expense for intangible assets

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"EBITDA Margin = EBITDA / Net Sales"
"EBITDA Margin = EBITDA / Revenue"
"EBITDA = Operating Income (EBIT) + Depreciation + Amortization"
earnings are "calculated by taking sales revenue and deducting operating expenses, such as cost of goods sold (COGS), selling, general, & administrative expenses (SG&A), but excluding depreciation and amortization."
