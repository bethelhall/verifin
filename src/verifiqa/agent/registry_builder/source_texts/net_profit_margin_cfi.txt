Source: Corporate Finance Institute (CFI)
URL: https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/accounting/net-profit-margin/
Metric: Net Profit Margin

PRIMARY FORMULA:
Net Profit Margin = Net Profit / Total Revenue x 100

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Net Profit: The amount remaining after deducting all company expenses from total revenue. Also referred to as "net income."
- Total Revenue: The total sales generated by the company during a specific period.
- Result: Expressed as a percentage, indicating the profit earned per dollar of revenue.

ALTERNATIVE FORMULA ARRANGEMENTS:
- To find net profit: Net Profit = Net Margin x Revenue
- To find revenue: Revenue = Net Profit / Net Profit Margin

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"Net Profit Margin = Net Profit / Total Revenue x 100"
"a 10% profit margin means for each $1 of revenue the company earns $0.10 in net profit."

KEY NOTES:
Net Profit Margin measures "the percentage of profit a company produces from its total revenue" and represents "the amount of net profit a company obtains per dollar of revenue gained."
