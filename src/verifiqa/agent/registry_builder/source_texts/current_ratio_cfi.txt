Source: Corporate Finance Institute (CFI)
URL: https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/accounting/current-ratio/
Metric: Current Ratio

PRIMARY FORMULA:
Current Ratio = Current Assets / Current Liabilities

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Current Assets: The sum of all current assets (short-term assets convertible to cash within one year)
- Current Liabilities: The sum of all current liabilities (short-term obligations due within one year)

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"Current Ratio = Current Assets / Current Liabilities"
"The current ratio is a key liquidity ratio that measures the ability of the company to cover its short-term liabilities."

KEY NOTES:
Used to assess operations and financial solidity relative to outstanding debt, valuable for creditors, investors, and suppliers.
