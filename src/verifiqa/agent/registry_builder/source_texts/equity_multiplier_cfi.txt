Source: Corporate Finance Institute (CFI)
URL: https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/accounting/equity-multiplier/
Authority: CFA Institute, Financial Reporting and Analysis (DuPont decomposition)
Metric: Equity Multiplier

PRIMARY FORMULA:
Equity Multiplier = Total Assets / Total Shareholders' Equity

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Total Assets: The company's complete asset holdings, from the balance sheet
- Total Shareholders' Equity: The owner's stake in the company, from the balance sheet

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"Equity Multiplier = Total Assets / Total Shareholder's Equity"
"a leverage ratio that measures the portion of the company's assets that are financed by shareholder's equity"

KEY NOTES:
Component of the DuPont decomposition: ROE = Net Profit Margin × Asset Turnover × Equity Multiplier
A higher multiplier indicates greater financial leverage (more debt financing relative to equity).
