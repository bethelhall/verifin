Source: CFA Institute, Alternative Investments curriculum
Authority: CFA Institute Program Curriculum, Level 1 & 2, Real Estate Valuation
Metric: Capitalization Rate (Cap Rate)

PRIMARY FORMULA:
Cap Rate = Net Operating Income / Property Value

REARRANGED FOR VALUATION:
Property Value = Net Operating Income / Cap Rate

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Net Operating Income (NOI): Rental income minus operating expenses (excluding debt service, depreciation, taxes)
- Property Value: Current market value or purchase price of the property
- Result: Expressed as a percentage; represents the unleveraged yield on a real estate investment

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"Cap Rate = Net Operating Income / Property Value"
"$500,000 (NOI) / 5% (CAP rate) = $10,000,000 (property value)"
"represents the rate of return on a real estate investment property based on the income that the property is expected to generate."
