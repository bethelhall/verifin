Source: Corporate Finance Institute (CFI)
URL: https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/real-estate/net-operating-income/
Authority: CFA Institute, Alternative Investments curriculum
Metric: Net Operating Income (NOI) — Real Estate

PRIMARY FORMULA:
NOI = Total Rental Income - Operating Expenses

EXTENDED FORMULA:
NOI = Gross Rental Income - Vacancy Allowance - Operating Expenses
(where Operating Expenses exclude debt service, depreciation, and income taxes)

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Total Rental Income: All revenue generated by the property
- Vacancy Allowance: Expressed as a percentage of rental income, simulating downtime where the property may sit vacant
- Operating Expenses: Include property taxes, utilities, insurance, repairs, maintenance, management fees (excludes debt service, depreciation, capital expenditures)

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"Total Rental Income – Total Expenses = NIBT"
"$500,000 (NOI) / 5% (CAP rate) = $10,000,000 (property value)"
"a normalized operating profit metric used to understand the economic value of a commercial or industrial investment property"

KEY NOTES:
NOI excludes financing costs and taxes, making it a property-level metric independent of capital structure.
Used as numerator in Cap Rate = NOI / Property Value.
