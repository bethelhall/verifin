Source: Corporate Finance Institute (CFI)
URL: https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/career/net-interest-margin/
Authority: CFA Institute, Financial Institutions analysis
Metric: Net Interest Margin (NIM)

PRIMARY FORMULA:
Net Interest Margin = (Interest Revenue - Interest Expense) / Average Earning Assets

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Interest Revenue: "Interest revenue is generated through interest payments the bank receives on outstanding loans. It is made up of credit lines and loans on the financial institution's balance sheet."
- Interest Expense: "The price the lender charges the borrower in a financing transaction. It is the cost of borrowing money. It is the interest that accumulates on outstanding liabilities."
- Average Earning Assets: Average of beginning and ending earning asset balances for the period

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"Net Interest Margin = (Interest Revenue - Interest Expense) / Average Earning Assets"
"Net interest margin is the difference between the interest income generated and the amount of interest paid out to lenders."
"an industry-specific profitability ratio for banks and other financial institutions that lend out interest-earning assets"
