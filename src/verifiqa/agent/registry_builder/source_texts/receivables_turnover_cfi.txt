Source: Corporate Finance Institute (CFI)
URL: https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/accounting/accounts-receivable-turnover-ratio/
Metric: Accounts Receivable Turnover Ratio / Receivables Turnover

PRIMARY FORMULA:
Accounts Receivable Turnover Ratio = Net Credit Sales / Average Accounts Receivable

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Net Credit Sales: Sales on credit - Sales returns - Sales allowances
- Average Accounts Receivable: (Opening Accounts Receivable + Closing Accounts Receivable) / 2

ALTERNATIVE FORMULA:
Receivable Turnover in Days = 365 / Receivable Turnover Ratio

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"Accounts Receivable Turnover Ratio = Net Credit Sales / Average Accounts Receivable"
"measures the number of times over a given period that a company collects its average accounts receivable."
