Source: CFA Institute, Financial Institutions analysis
Authority: CFA Institute Program Curriculum, Level 1 & 2, Insurance Companies
Metric: Loss Ratio (Insurance)

PRIMARY FORMULA:
Loss Ratio = (Losses Incurred + Loss Adjustment Expenses) / Earned Premiums

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Losses Incurred: Total claims paid or payable to policyholders during the period
- Loss Adjustment Expenses (LAE): Expenses to investigate, manage, and settle insurance claims
- Earned Premiums: The portion of written premiums recognized as revenue during the period

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"Loss Ratio = (Losses Incurred + LAE) / Earned Premiums"
"measures the percentage of earned premiums paid out as claims. A lower ratio indicates better underwriting performance."
