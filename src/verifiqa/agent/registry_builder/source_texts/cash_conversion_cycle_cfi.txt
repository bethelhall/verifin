Source: Corporate Finance Institute (CFI)
URL: https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/accounting/cash-conversion-cycle/
Authority: CFA Institute, Financial Reporting and Analysis
Metric: Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC)

PRIMARY FORMULA:
CCC = DIO + DSO - DPO

COMPONENT FORMULAS:
DIO = (Average Inventory / Cost of Sales) × Days
DSO = (Accounts Receivable / Net Credit Sales) × Days
DPO = (Average Accounts Payable / Cost of Goods Sold) × Days

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- DIO (Days Inventory Outstanding): "the number of days, on average, it takes a company to turn its inventory into sales"
- DSO (Days Sales Outstanding): "the number of days, on average, it takes a company to collect its receivables"
- DPO (Days Payable Outstanding): "the number of days, on average, it takes a company to pay back its payables"

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"Cash Conversion Cycle = DIO + DSO – DPO"
"The Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC) is a metric that shows the amount of time it takes a company to convert its investments in inventory to cash"
"CCC = 18.25 + 15.20 – 13.69 = 19.76"
