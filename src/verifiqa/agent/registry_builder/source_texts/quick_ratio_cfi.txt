Source: Corporate Finance Institute (CFI)
URL: https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/accounting/quick-ratio/
Metric: Quick Ratio / Acid Test Ratio

PRIMARY FORMULA:
Quick Ratio = [Cash & Equivalents + Marketable Securities + Accounts Receivable] / Current Liabilities

ALTERNATIVE FORMULA:
Quick Ratio = [Current Assets - Inventory - Prepaid Expenses] / Current Liabilities

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Cash and Cash Equivalents: Most liquid assets
- Marketable Securities: Short-term investments readily convertible to cash
- Accounts Receivable: Amounts owed by customers
- Inventory: Excluded — takes too long to convert to cash
- Prepaid Expenses: Excluded — cannot be used to pay liabilities
- Current Liabilities: Short-term obligations due within one year

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"Quick Ratio = [Cash & Equivalents + Marketable Securities + Accounts Receivable] / Current Liabilities"
"Quick Ratio = [Current Assets - Inventory - Prepaid expenses] / Current Liabilities"
"measures a business's ability to pay its short-term liabilities by using assets that are readily convertible into cash."
