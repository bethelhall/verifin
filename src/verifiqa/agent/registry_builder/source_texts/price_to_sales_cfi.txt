Source: Corporate Finance Institute (CFI)
URL: https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/valuation/price-to-sales-ratio/
Authority: CFA Institute, Equity Investments curriculum
Metric: Price-to-Sales Ratio (P/S)

PRIMARY FORMULA:
P/S = Share Price / Sales Per Share

ALTERNATIVE FORMULA:
P/S = Market Capitalization / Total Revenue

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Share Price: Current market price per share
- Sales Per Share: Total Revenue / Shares Outstanding
- Market Capitalization: Share price multiplied by total shares outstanding
- Total Revenue (Sales): Annual revenue from the income statement

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"It is calculated by dividing the share price by the sales per share."
"The Price to Sales ratio, also known as the P/S ratio, is a formula used to measure the total value that investors place on the company in comparison to the total revenue generated by the business."
