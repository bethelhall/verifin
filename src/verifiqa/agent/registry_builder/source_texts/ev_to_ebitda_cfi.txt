Source: Corporate Finance Institute (CFI)
URL: https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/valuation/ev-ebitda/
Authority: CFA Institute, Financial Reporting and Analysis; Damodaran, Investment Valuation
Metric: EV/EBITDA (Enterprise Value to EBITDA)

PRIMARY FORMULA:
EV/EBITDA = Enterprise Value / EBITDA

COMPONENT FORMULA:
Enterprise Value (EV) = Market Capitalization + Total Debt - Cash and Cash Equivalents
EBITDA = Operating Income (EBIT) + Depreciation + Amortization

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Enterprise Value: "A firm's EV is equal to its equity value (or market capitalization) plus its debt (or financial commitments) less any cash."
- EBITDA: "Earnings Before Interest Taxes Depreciation and Amortization. It is often used in valuation as a proxy for cash flow."

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"by taking the EV column and dividing it by the EBITDA column, one arrives at the EV/EBITDA column."
"The ratio of EV/EBITDA is used to compare the entire value of a business with the amount of EBITDA it earns on an annual basis."
