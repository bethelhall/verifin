Source: Corporate Finance Institute (CFI)
URL: https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/accounting/what-is-free-cash-flow-fcf/
Metric: Free Cash Flow (FCF)

PRIMARY FORMULA:
Free Cash Flow = Cash from Operations - CapEx

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Cash from Operations (CFO): Cash generated from a company's normal business operations
- CapEx (Capital Expenditures): Purchases of assets such as property, equipment, and other major investments

EXPANDED COMPONENT FORMULAS:
CFO = Net Income + Non-Cash Expenses - Increase in Non-Cash Net Working Capital
Non-Cash Adjustments = Depreciation + Amortization + Stock-Based Compensation + Impairment Charges +/- Losses/Gains on Investments
CapEx = Year 2 PP&E - Year 1 PP&E + Depreciation
Changes in Working Capital = (Year 2 AR - Year 1 AR) + (Year 2 Inventory - Year 1 Inventory) - (Year 2 AP - Year 1 AP)
Where: AR = Accounts Receivable, AP = Accounts Payable, PP&E = Property, Plant & Equipment

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"Free Cash Flow = Cash from Operations - CapEx"
"FCF = Net Income + [Depreciation + Amortization + Stock-Based Compensation + Impairment Charges +/- Losses/Gains on Investments] - [(Year 2 AR - Year 1 AR) + (Year 2 Inventory - Year 1 Inventory) - (Year 2 AP - Year 1 AP)] - [Year 2 PP&E - Year 1 PP&E + Depreciation]"
