Source: Corporate Finance Institute (CFI)
URL: https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/accounting/price-earnings-ratio/
Metric: Price to Earnings Ratio (P/E Ratio)

PRIMARY FORMULA:
P/E = Stock Price Per Share / Earnings Per Share

ALTERNATIVE FORMULAS:
P/E = Market Capitalization / Total Net Earnings
Justified P/E = Dividend Payout Ratio / (R - G)

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Stock Price Per Share: Current market price of one share
- Earnings Per Share (EPS): Earnings from the last twelve months divided by weighted average shares outstanding
- Market Capitalization: Total market value of all outstanding shares
- Total Net Earnings: Company's total net income
- R: Required Rate of Return
- G: Sustainable Growth Rate

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"P/E = Stock Price Per Share / Earnings Per Share"
"P/E = Market Capitalization / Total Net Earnings"
"Justified P/E = Dividend Payout Ratio / R - G"
"The P/E ratio is equal to the current stock price divided by EPS"
