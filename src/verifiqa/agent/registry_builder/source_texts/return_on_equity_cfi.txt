Source: Corporate Finance Institute (CFI)
URL: https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/accounting/return-on-equity/
Metric: Return on Equity (ROE)

PRIMARY FORMULA:
ROE = Net Income / Shareholder's Equity

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Net Income: A company's annual profit or earnings
- Shareholder's Equity: The total value of shareholders' equity (also called stockholders' equity)

ALTERNATIVE FORMULA:
ROE can also be derived by dividing the firm's dividend growth rate by its earnings retention rate (or, 1 - dividend payout ratio).

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"ROE is a measure of a company's annual return (net income) divided by the value of its total shareholders' equity (i.e. 12%)."
"ROE = Net Income / Shareholder's Equity"

KEY NOTES:
ROE measures the total return on equity capital and shows the firm's ability to turn equity into profits, or the profits made for each dollar from shareholders' equity.
