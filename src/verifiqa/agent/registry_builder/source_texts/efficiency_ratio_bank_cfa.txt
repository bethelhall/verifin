Source: CFA Institute, Financial Institutions analysis
Authority: CFA Institute Program Curriculum, Level 1 & 2, Financial Institutions
Metric: Bank Efficiency Ratio

PRIMARY FORMULA:
Efficiency Ratio = Non-Interest Expense / Revenue

ALTERNATIVE FORMULA:
Efficiency Ratio = Non-Interest Expense / (Net Interest Income + Non-Interest Income)

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Non-Interest Expense: Operating expenses excluding interest expense (salaries, occupancy, technology, etc.)
- Revenue: Total revenue = Net Interest Income + Non-Interest Income
- Net Interest Income: Interest earned on loans minus interest paid on deposits
- Non-Interest Income: Fees, commissions, trading income, service charges

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"Efficiency Ratio = Non-Interest Expense / Revenue"
"measures how much of a bank's revenue is consumed by operating expenses. A lower ratio indicates greater efficiency."
"A ratio below 50% is considered excellent; above 70% is considered poor."
