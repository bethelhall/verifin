Source: CFA Institute, Equity Investments curriculum
Authority: CFA Institute Program Curriculum, Level 1 & 2, Equity Valuation
Metric: EV/Revenue (Enterprise Value to Revenue)

PRIMARY FORMULA:
EV/Revenue = Enterprise Value / Total Revenue

COMPONENT FORMULA:
Enterprise Value = Market Capitalization + Total Debt - Cash and Cash Equivalents

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Enterprise Value: Total value of the business to all capital providers (debt and equity holders)
- Total Revenue: Annual net revenue (net sales) from the income statement
- Result: A multiple indicating how many times revenue the market values the business at

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"EV/Revenue = Enterprise Value / Revenue"
"EV/Revenue is a valuation multiple that compares the total value of a company to its annual revenue."

KEY NOTES:
Used when EBITDA or earnings are negative (e.g., early-stage companies).
Lower multiples suggest cheaper valuation relative to revenue.
Also called EV/Sales or Price-to-Sales on an enterprise basis.
