Source: Corporate Finance Institute (CFI)
URL: https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/accounting/inventory-turnover/
Metric: Inventory Turnover Ratio

PRIMARY FORMULA:
Inventory Turnover Ratio = Cost of Goods Sold / Average Inventory

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Cost of Goods Sold (COGS): The expense incurred from directly creating a product, including raw materials and labor costs. For merchandising businesses, the actual amount paid for finished products.
- Average Inventory: The average cost of goods during the period, calculated as (Beginning Inventory + Ending Inventory) / 2.

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"Inventory Turnover Ratio = (Cost of Goods Sold) / (Average Inventory)"
"the number of times a business sells and replaces its stock of goods during a given period."

RELATED FORMULA:
Inventory Turnover Days = (Average Inventory / COGS) x 365
