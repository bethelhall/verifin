Source: Corporate Finance Institute (CFI)
URL: https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/accounting/debt-service-coverage-ratio/
Authority: CFA Institute, Fixed Income curriculum
Metric: Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)

PRIMARY FORMULA:
DSCR = EBITDA / (Principal + Interest + Cash Taxes)

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- EBITDA: Earnings Before Interest, Tax, Depreciation, and Amortization
- Principal: The total amount of loan principal due within the measurement period (current portion of long-term debt)
- Interest: Total aggregate interest due within the measurement period on both current and non-current portions of long-term debt
- Cash Taxes: The proportion of total income tax due in cash during the current measurement period

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"DSCR = EBITDA / (Principal + Interest + Cash Taxes)"
"Debt Service Coverage is usually calculated using EBITDA as a proxy for cash flow."
