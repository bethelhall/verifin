Source: Corporate Finance Institute (CFI)
URL: https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/accounting/days-sales-outstanding/
Authority: CFA Institute, Financial Reporting and Analysis
Metric: Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)

PRIMARY FORMULA:
DSO = (Accounts Receivable / Net Credit Sales) × Number of Days

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Accounts Receivable: Balance of money owed by customers for credit purchases during the period
- Net Credit Sales: Revenue generated exclusively through credit transactions (excludes cash sales)
- Number of Days: The timeframe measured (365 for annual, 90 for quarterly, etc.)

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"DSO = Accounts Receivables / Net Credit Sales X Number of Days"
"Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) represents the average number of days it takes credit sales to be converted into cash or how long it takes a company to collect its account receivables."
"cash sales are not included in the computation because they are considered a zero DSO"
