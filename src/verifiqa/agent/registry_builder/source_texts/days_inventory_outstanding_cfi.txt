Source: Corporate Finance Institute (CFI)
URL: https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/accounting/days-inventory-outstanding/
Authority: CFA Institute, Financial Reporting and Analysis
Metric: Days Inventory Outstanding (DIO)

PRIMARY FORMULA:
DIO = (Average Inventory / Cost of Sales) × Number of Days

COMPONENT FORMULA:
Average Inventory = (Beginning Inventory + Ending Inventory) / 2

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Average Inventory: Average of beginning and ending inventory balances
- Cost of Sales: Also referred to as Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
- Number of Days: 365 for annual, 90 for quarterly

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"Days Inventory Outstanding = (Average Inventory / Cost of Sales) x Number of Days in Period"
"the average number of days that a company holds its inventory before selling it"
