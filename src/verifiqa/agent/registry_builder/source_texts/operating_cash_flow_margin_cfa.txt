Source: CFA Institute, Financial Reporting and Analysis curriculum
Authority: CFA Institute Program Curriculum, Level 1, Cash Flow Statement Analysis
Metric: Operating Cash Flow Margin

PRIMARY FORMULA:
Operating Cash Flow Margin = Operating Cash Flow / Net Revenue

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Operating Cash Flow (CFO): Cash generated from operating activities, from the cash flow statement
- Net Revenue: Total revenue (net sales) from the income statement
- Result: Expressed as a percentage; indicates cents of operating cash flow per dollar of revenue

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"Operating Cash Flow Margin = Cash Flow from Operations / Net Revenue"
"measures the cash generated from operations as a proportion of revenue, reflecting actual cash profitability rather than accounting profit."

KEY NOTES:
Differs from operating profit margin in that it uses actual cash flow (before non-cash adjustments) rather than EBIT.
A higher ratio relative to net profit margin suggests high-quality earnings with strong cash conversion.
