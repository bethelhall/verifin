Source: CFA Institute, Financial Institutions analysis
Authority: CFA Institute Program Curriculum, Level 1 & 2, Insurance Companies
Metric: Combined Ratio (Insurance)

PRIMARY FORMULA:
Combined Ratio = Loss Ratio + Expense Ratio

COMPONENT FORMULAS:
Loss Ratio = (Losses Incurred + Loss Adjustment Expenses) / Earned Premiums
Expense Ratio = Underwriting Expenses / Written Premiums

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Losses Incurred: Claims paid and outstanding to policyholders
- Loss Adjustment Expenses (LAE): Costs to investigate and settle claims
- Earned Premiums: Portion of written premiums applicable to the current period
- Underwriting Expenses: Agent commissions, administrative costs, marketing
- Written Premiums: Total premiums on policies written during the period

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"Combined Ratio = Loss Ratio + Expense Ratio"
"A combined ratio below 100% indicates underwriting profitability; above 100% indicates an underwriting loss."
"Combined Ratio = (Losses Incurred + LAE + Underwriting Expenses) / Earned Premiums"
