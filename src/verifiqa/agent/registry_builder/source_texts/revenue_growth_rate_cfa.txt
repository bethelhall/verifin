Source: CFA Institute, Financial Reporting and Analysis curriculum
Authority: CFA Institute Program Curriculum, Level 1, Common-Size and Growth Analysis
Metric: Revenue Growth Rate

PRIMARY FORMULA:
Revenue Growth Rate = (Current Period Revenue - Prior Period Revenue) / Prior Period Revenue × 100

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Current Period Revenue: Net revenue for the current reporting period
- Prior Period Revenue: Net revenue for the comparison period (prior year or prior quarter)
- Result: Expressed as a percentage; positive values indicate growth, negative values indicate contraction

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"Revenue Growth Rate = (Revenue_t - Revenue_t-1) / Revenue_t-1 × 100"
"measures the percentage increase or decrease in a company's sales from one period to the next."

KEY NOTES:
Can be computed year-over-year (YoY) or quarter-over-quarter (QoQ).
For multi-period analysis, use CAGR = (End Value / Start Value)^(1/n) - 1.
