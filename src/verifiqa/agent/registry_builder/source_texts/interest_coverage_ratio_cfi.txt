Source: Corporate Finance Institute (CFI)
URL: https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/accounting/interest-coverage-ratio/
Metric: Interest Coverage Ratio / Times Interest Earned

PRIMARY FORMULA:
Interest Coverage Ratio = EBIT / Interest Expense

ALTERNATIVE FORMULA:
Interest Coverage Ratio = EBITDA / Interest Expense

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- EBIT: Earnings Before Interest and Taxes — the company's operating profit
- EBITDA: Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation and Amortization
- Interest Expense: The interest payable on any borrowings such as bonds, loans, lines of credit, etc.

COMPONENT FORMULA:
EBIT = Revenue - COGS - Operating Expenses

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"Interest Coverage Ratio = EBIT / Interest Expense"
"Interest Coverage Ratio = EBITDA / Interest Expense"
"the company's operating profit (Earnings Before Interest and Taxes)"
"the interest payable on any borrowings such as bonds, loans, lines of credit, etc."
