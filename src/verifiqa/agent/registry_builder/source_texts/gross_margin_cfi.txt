Source: Corporate Finance Institute (CFI)
URL: https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/accounting/gross-margin/
Metric: Gross Margin / Gross Profit Margin

PRIMARY FORMULA:
Gross Margin Ratio = (Revenue - COGS) / Revenue

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Revenue: Total sales generated by the company
- COGS: Cost of Goods Sold, the direct costs to produce goods
- Result: Expressed as a percentage

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"Gross Margin Ratio = (Revenue - COGS) / Revenue"
"= (102,007 - 39,023) / 102,007 = 0.6174 (61.74%)"

KEY NOTES:
The metric indicates "the percentage of each dollar of revenue that the company retains as gross profit."
It measures "how much profit a company makes after paying off its Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)."
