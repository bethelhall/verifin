Source: Corporate Finance Institute (CFI)
URL: https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/accounting/return-on-assets-roa-formula/
Metric: Return on Assets (ROA)

PRIMARY FORMULA:
ROA = (Net Income / Average Total Assets) x 100

ALTERNATIVE FORMULA:
ROA = Net Income / Ending Total Assets

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Net Income: Net earnings for the period (typically annual), sourced from the income statement
- Average Total Assets: Calculated as (Beginning Total Assets + Ending Total Assets) / 2, derived from the balance sheet
- Ending Total Assets: Total assets at end of period (used when averaging is not performed)

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"ROA = (Net Income / Average Total Assets) x 100"
"ROA = Net Income / Ending Total Assets"

KEY NOTES:
"Using average assets provides a more accurate measure when asset levels change significantly during the period."
A company generating $10 million in net income with $50 million in total assets would achieve a 20% ROA, indicating the business produces $0.20 in profit per dollar of assets owned.
