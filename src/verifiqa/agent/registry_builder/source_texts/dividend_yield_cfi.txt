Source: Corporate Finance Institute (CFI)
URL: https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/accounting/dividend-yield-formula/
Authority: CFA Institute, Financial Reporting and Analysis
Metric: Dividend Yield

PRIMARY FORMULA:
Dividend Yield = Dividend Per Share / Market Value Per Share

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Dividend Per Share: "the company's total annual dividend payment, divided by the total number of shares outstanding"
- Market Value Per Share: "the current share price of the company"
- Result: Expressed as a percentage

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"Dividend Yield = Dividend per share / Market value per share"
"a financial ratio that measures the annual value of dividends received relative to the market value per share of a security"
"calculates the percentage of a company's market price of a share that is paid to shareholders in the form of dividends"
