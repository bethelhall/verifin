Source: Corporate Finance Institute (CFI)
URL: https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/accounting/net-debt-ebitda-ratio/
Authority: CFA Institute, Financial Reporting and Analysis
Metric: Net Debt to EBITDA Ratio

PRIMARY FORMULA:
Net Debt to EBITDA = Net Debt / EBITDA

COMPONENT FORMULAS:
Net Debt = Total Debt - Cash and Cash Equivalents
Total Debt = Short-Term Debt + Long-Term Debt
EBITDA = Operating Income + Depreciation + Amortization

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Net Debt: "Total debt minus cash and cash equivalents, thereby accounting for assets that are immediately available to repay senior creditors"
- EBITDA: "The sum of the company's earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization expenses from its income statement." Functions as "a common proxy for operating cash flow"

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"Net Debt-to-EBITDA Ratio = Net Debt / EBITDA"
"a leverage ratio that compares a company's liabilities in the form of net debt to its 'cash flow,' in the form of EBITDA"
