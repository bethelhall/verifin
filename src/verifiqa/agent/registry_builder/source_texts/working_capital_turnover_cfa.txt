Source: CFA Institute, Financial Reporting and Analysis curriculum
Authority: CFA Institute Program Curriculum, Level 1, Activity Ratios
Metric: Working Capital Turnover Ratio

PRIMARY FORMULA:
Working Capital Turnover = Net Revenue / Average Working Capital

COMPONENT FORMULA:
Working Capital = Current Assets - Current Liabilities
Average Working Capital = (Beginning Working Capital + Ending Working Capital) / 2

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Net Revenue: Net sales for the period
- Working Capital: Current assets minus current liabilities
- Average Working Capital: Average of beginning and ending working capital balances

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"Working Capital Turnover = Net Revenue / Working Capital"
"measures how efficiently a company uses its working capital to generate revenue."
"A higher ratio indicates more efficient use of working capital."
