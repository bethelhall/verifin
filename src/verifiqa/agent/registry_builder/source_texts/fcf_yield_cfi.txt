Source: Corporate Finance Institute (CFI)
URL: https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/valuation/free-cash-flow-yield/
Authority: CFA Institute, Equity Investments curriculum
Metric: Free Cash Flow Yield

PRIMARY FORMULA:
FCF Yield = Free Cash Flow / Market Capitalization

COMPONENT FORMULA:
Free Cash Flow = Operating Cash Flow - Capital Expenditures

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Free Cash Flow: Operating cash flow minus capital expenditures
- Market Capitalization: Share price multiplied by shares outstanding

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"free cash flow yield is determined, first, by using a company's cash flow statement, subtracting capital expenditures from all cash flow operations. Then, the free cash flow value is divided by the company's value or market cap."
"Free cash flow yield is important for any business, large or small, because it acts as a good metric for cash flow in comparison to the company's size."
