Source: CFA Institute, Equity Investments curriculum
Authority: CFA Institute Program Curriculum, Level 1, Equity Valuation
Metric: Price-to-Book Ratio (P/B)

PRIMARY FORMULA:
P/B = Market Price Per Share / Book Value Per Share

ALTERNATIVE FORMULA:
P/B = Market Capitalization / Total Book Value of Equity

COMPONENT FORMULA:
Book Value Per Share = (Total Shareholders' Equity - Preferred Stock) / Common Shares Outstanding

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Market Price Per Share: Current market price of one common share
- Book Value Per Share: Net asset value per common share from the balance sheet
- Market Capitalization: Total market value of all outstanding common shares
- Total Book Value of Equity: Total shareholders' equity minus preferred stock, from the balance sheet

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"P/B = Market Price Per Share / Book Value Per Share"
"Market Cap / Total Book Value = P/B ratio"
"A P/B ratio below 1.0 may indicate that shares are trading below the net asset value of the company."
"A higher P/B ratio implies that investors expect management to create more value from a given set of assets."

KEY NOTES:
Also known as: market-to-book ratio, price-to-equity ratio.
Inverse (B/P) is called the book-to-market ratio.
