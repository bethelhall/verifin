Source: CFA Institute, Financial Reporting and Analysis curriculum
Authority: CFA Institute Program Curriculum, Level 1, Solvency Ratios
Metric: Debt Ratio (Total Debt Ratio)

PRIMARY FORMULA:
Debt Ratio = Total Liabilities / Total Assets

ALTERNATIVE EXPRESSION:
Debt Ratio = Total Debt / Total Assets

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Total Liabilities: All obligations of the company (current and non-current liabilities)
- Total Assets: All assets of the company (current and non-current)
- Result: Expressed as a ratio or percentage; indicates the proportion of assets financed by debt

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"Debt Ratio = Total Liabilities / Total Assets"
"measures the proportion of a company's assets that are financed by debt. A higher ratio indicates greater financial leverage and risk."
"A debt ratio above 0.5 means more than half of the company's assets are financed by liabilities."
