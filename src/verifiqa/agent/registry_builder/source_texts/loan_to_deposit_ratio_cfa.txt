Source: CFA Institute, Financial Institutions analysis
Authority: CFA Institute Program Curriculum, Level 1 & 2, Financial Institutions
Metric: Loan-to-Deposit Ratio (LDR)

PRIMARY FORMULA:
LDR = Total Loans / Total Deposits

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Total Loans: Total outstanding loan balances extended by the bank to borrowers
- Total Deposits: Total customer deposits held by the bank (demand deposits, savings, time deposits)
- Result: Expressed as a ratio or percentage; higher values indicate greater use of deposits for lending

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"Loan-to-Deposit Ratio = Total Loans / Total Deposits"
"measures a bank's liquidity by comparing total loans to total deposits. A higher ratio indicates the bank is lending out more of its deposits."

KEY NOTES:
Regulators monitor LDR as a liquidity risk indicator.
Too high (above ~100%) signals potential liquidity stress; too low signals under-deployment of capital.
