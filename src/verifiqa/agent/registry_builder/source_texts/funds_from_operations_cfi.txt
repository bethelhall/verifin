Source: Corporate Finance Institute (CFI)
URL: https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/real-estate/funds-from-operations/
Authority: CFA Institute, Alternative Investments curriculum; NAREIT definition
Metric: Funds From Operations (FFO) — REIT

PRIMARY FORMULA:
FFO = Net Income + Depreciation Expense + Amortization Expense + Losses on Sale of Assets - Gains on Sale of Assets - Interest Income

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Net Income: The company's profit after all expenses and taxes
- Depreciation Expense: "A non-cash allocation covering capital expenditures for fixed assets"
- Amortization Expense: Loan and capital expense payments spread over a specific period
- Losses on Sale of Assets: Occurs when an asset sells below its net book value (added back)
- Gains on Sale of Assets: Profit when an asset sells above net book value (subtracted)
- Interest Income: Earnings from marketable securities or interest-bearing accounts (subtracted)

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"FFO = Net Income + (Depreciation Expense + Amortization Expense + Losses on Sale of Assets) – (Gains on Sale of Assets + Interest Income)"
"the actual amount of cash flow generated from a company's business operations"
