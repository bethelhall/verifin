Source: Corporate Finance Institute (CFI)
URL: https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/accounting/operating-profit-margin/
Metric: Operating Margin / Operating Profit Margin

PRIMARY FORMULA:
Operating Margin = Operating Profit / Total Revenue

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Operating Profit: Calculated by subtracting COGS, depreciation, amortization, and all operating expenses from total revenues. Also known as EBIT (Earnings Before Interest and Tax).
- Total Revenue: The company's overall sales revenue
- Operating Expenses: Costs beyond direct production, including salaries, benefits, rent, overhead, and R&D

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"Operating profit divided by total revenue, expressed as a percentage"
"The margin is also known as the EBIT (Earnings Before Interest and Tax) Margin."
"A 15% operating profit margin is equal to $0.15 operating profit for every $1 of revenue."

KEY NOTES:
Operating profit margin differs from net profit margin because it excludes financing costs (interest payments) and taxes, focusing solely on operational performance.
Operating Profit = Revenue - COGS - Operating Expenses - Depreciation - Amortization
