Source: CFA Institute, Financial Reporting and Analysis curriculum
Authority: CFA Institute Program Curriculum, Level 1
Metric: Dividends Per Share (DPS)

PRIMARY FORMULA:
DPS = Total Dividends Paid to Common Shareholders / Weighted Average Common Shares Outstanding

ALTERNATIVE FORMULA:
DPS = Annual Dividend Per Share (as declared by the board)

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Total Dividends Paid to Common Shareholders: Total cash dividends paid during the period (excludes preferred dividends)
- Weighted Average Common Shares Outstanding: Average shares outstanding during the period weighted by time

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"Dividends Per Share = Total Dividends Paid / Shares Outstanding"
"the sum of declared dividends issued by a company for every ordinary share outstanding."

KEY NOTES:
DPS represents the actual cash returned to common shareholders per share.
Trailing DPS uses actual dividends paid; forward DPS uses declared or expected dividends.
