Source: CFA Institute, Financial Reporting and Analysis curriculum
Authority: CFA Institute Program Curriculum, Level 1; IAS 33; ASC 260
Metric: Earnings Per Share (EPS)

PRIMARY FORMULA (Basic EPS):
Basic EPS = (Net Income - Preferred Dividends) / Weighted Average Common Shares Outstanding

DILUTED EPS FORMULA:
Diluted EPS = (Net Income - Preferred Dividends + Convertible Preferred Dividends + After-tax Interest on Convertible Debt) / (Weighted Average Shares + Dilutive Potential Shares)

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Net Income: Company's total earnings for the period
- Preferred Dividends: Dividends owed to preferred shareholders (subtracted because they have priority over common)
- Weighted Average Common Shares Outstanding: Average shares outstanding during the period, weighted by the fraction of the period they were outstanding
- Dilutive Potential Shares: Additional shares from convertible securities, stock options, and warrants (for diluted EPS)

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"Basic EPS = (Net Income - Preferred Dividends) / Weighted Average Common Shares Outstanding"
"Diluted EPS reflects the 'worst case' scenario for earnings per share, assuming all dilutive securities are converted."
"EPS is the monetary value of earnings per outstanding share of common stock for a company during a defined period of time."
