Source: CFA Institute, Financial Reporting and Analysis curriculum
Authority: CFA Institute Program Curriculum, Level 1 & 2; Damodaran, Investment Valuation
Metric: Return on Invested Capital (ROIC)

PRIMARY FORMULA:
ROIC = NOPAT / Invested Capital

COMPONENT FORMULAS:
NOPAT = EBIT × (1 - Tax Rate)
Invested Capital = Total Equity + Total Debt - Cash and Cash Equivalents

ALTERNATIVE FORMULA:
ROIC = EBIT(1 - t) / (Book Value of Debt + Book Value of Equity - Cash)

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- NOPAT: Net Operating Profit After Tax — operating profit adjusted for taxes, representing returns to all capital providers
- EBIT: Earnings Before Interest and Taxes (Operating Income)
- Tax Rate: Effective tax rate
- Invested Capital: Total capital employed by debt and equity holders, net of excess cash
- Total Equity: Book value of shareholders' equity
- Total Debt: Short-term and long-term interest-bearing debt

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"ROIC = NOPAT / Invested Capital"
"ROIC = EBIT(1-t) / (Book Value of Debt + Book Value of Equity - Cash)"
"If the ROIC is greater than the WACC, then value is being created."
"Return on Invested Capital (ROIC) is a profitability or performance measure of the return earned by those who provide capital, namely the firm's bondholders and stockholders."
