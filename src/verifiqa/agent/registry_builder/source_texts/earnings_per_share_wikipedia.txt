Metric: Earnings Per Share (EPS)

PRIMARY FORMULA:
Earnings per share = (Net Income - Preferred Dividends) / Weighted Average Common Shares

ALTERNATIVE FORMULAS:
Basic EPS = (Profit - Preferred Dividends) / Weighted Average Common Shares
EPS from continuing operations = (Income from Continuing Operations - Preferred Dividends) / Weighted Average Common Shares

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Net Income / Profit: Company earnings for the period
- Preferred Dividends: Subtracted because preferred stockholders have priority over common stockholders
- Weighted Average Common Shares: Average number of common shares outstanding during the time period, weighted by time

DILUTED EPS:
Diluted EPS adjusts both numerator and denominator to account for potential share issuances from convertible securities, stock options, and warrants, reflecting a "worst case" scenario for dilution.

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"Earnings per share = profit - preferred dividends / weighted average common shares"
"Earnings per share = net income - preferred dividends / average common shares"
EPS represents "the monetary value of earnings per outstanding share of common stock for a company during a defined period of time, often a year."
