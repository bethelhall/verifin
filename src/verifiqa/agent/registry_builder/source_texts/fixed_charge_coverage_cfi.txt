Source: Corporate Finance Institute (CFI)
URL: https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/accounting/fixed-charge-coverage-ratio/
Authority: CFA Institute, Fixed Income curriculum
Metric: Fixed Charge Coverage Ratio (FCCR)

PRIMARY FORMULA:
FCCR = (EBIT + Fixed Charges Before Tax) / (Fixed Charges Before Tax + Interest)

COMMON SIMPLIFIED FORMULA:
FCCR = EBITDA / (Interest Expense + Lease Payments + Debt Principal)

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- EBIT: Earnings Before Interest and Taxes
- EBITDA: Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization
- Fixed Charges: Long-term debt repayments, interest expenses, lease payments
- Interest Expense: Interest payments on debt obligations

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"There is no 'standard' formula — lenders and borrowers negotiate specific adjustments within credit agreements."
"EBITDA is used as the numerator as a proxy for operating cash flow."

KEY NOTES:
The definition of fixed charges varies by credit agreement. The most common components are interest expense and lease/rental payments.
A ratio above 1.5x is generally considered adequate; below 1.0x indicates inability to cover fixed charges.
