Source: Corporate Finance Institute (CFI)
URL: https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/accounting/book-value-per-share/
Metric: Book Value Per Share (BVPS)

PRIMARY FORMULA:
BVPS = (Total Stockholders' Equity - Preferred Stock) / Average Common Shares Outstanding

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Total Stockholders' Equity: Total equity of the company
- Preferred Stock: Must be excluded because preferred stockholders rank higher during liquidation
- Average Common Shares Outstanding: Average number of common shares during the period (not period-end, to avoid distortion from stock issuances or buybacks)

EXAMPLE CALCULATION:
BVPS = ($20,000,000 - $5,000,000) / 3,000,000 = $5.00 per share

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"BVPS = (Total Stockholders' Equity - Preferred Stock) / Average Common Shares Outstanding"
"the ratio of equity available to common stockholders against the number of shares outstanding."
"the preferred stock should be excluded from the value of equity"
