Source: Corporate Finance Institute (CFI)
URL: https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/accounting/days-payable-outstanding/
Authority: CFA Institute, Financial Reporting and Analysis
Metric: Days Payable Outstanding (DPO)

PRIMARY FORMULA:
DPO = (Average Accounts Payable / Cost of Goods Sold) × Number of Days

ALTERNATIVE FORMULA:
DPO = Average Accounts Payable / (Cost of Sales / Number of Days)

COMPONENT FORMULA:
Cost of Sales = Beginning Inventory + Purchases - Ending Inventory

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS:
- Average Accounts Payable: Average of beginning and ending accounts payable balances
- Cost of Goods Sold (COGS): Direct cost of goods sold during the period
- Number of Days: 365 for annual, 90 for quarterly

VERBATIM FORMULA STATEMENTS:
"Days Payable Outstanding = (Average Accounts Payable / Cost of Goods Sold) x Number of Days"
"the average number of days it takes a company to pay back its accounts payable"
